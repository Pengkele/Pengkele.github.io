    Professional experience

    Senior R&D Engineer

    X-MAGTECH · Beijing, China

    March 2021–Present

    I develop software and algorithms for cryogen-free magnetoencephalography, with responsibilities spanning data acquisition, neuroimaging analysis, product development, and clinical implementation.

    • Software and algorithms. Develop OPM-MEG acquisition software and clinical and research analysis tools, including signal processing, source localization, and multimodal neuroimaging workflows.
    • Product development. Contribute to the development of the Marvel MEG product family and selected magnetocardiography products, from research prototypes to deployed systems.
    • Registration and quality. Support medical device registration across the Marvel MEG product family, software development within quality management processes, and Class II medical device clinical studies.
    • Data analysis and training. Process and analyze electrophysiological and imaging data, provide technical support, deliver MEG data-analysis training, and contribute to research manuscripts.
    • Systems integration. Develop and maintain medical equipment network services, implement interfaces, and support device connectivity and software deployment in hospital networks.

    Research projects

    • Core project contributor to the development of a cryogen-free MEG system under China’s National Key R&D Program during the 14th Five-Year Plan, including proposal preparation and project implementation.
    • Contributor to two Beijing Natural Science Foundation projects during doctoral study.

    Technical skills

    • Programming: Python, MATLAB; working knowledge of R, machine learning and deep learning frameworks.
    • Neuroimaging: Brainstorm, FieldTrip, MNE, SPM, ANTsPy, 3D Slicer; familiarity with FreeSurfer, CONN, and FSL.
    • Methods: MEG/EEG/SEEG/MRI analysis, ERP/ERF, time–frequency analysis, aperiodic activity, functional connectivity, graph analysis, microstates, network dynamics, source localization, simulation, and 3D registration.
