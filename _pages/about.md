I am a PhD researcher at **Beihang University** and a **Senior R&D Engineer at X-MAGTECH**. My work focuses on cryogen-free magnetoencephalography (OPM-MEG), from acquisition software and signal-processing algorithms to clinical and research applications.

My research combines MEG, EEG, SEEG, and MRI to investigate epileptogenic sources and brain networks, with broader interests in functional mapping and the clinical translation of neuroimaging technology.

## Research interests

- **OPM-MEG systems:** acquisition software, sensor configurations, simulation, and system evaluation.
- **Brain signals and networks:** source imaging, electrophysiological analysis, and multimodal neuroimaging.
- **Clinical translation:** epilepsy evaluation, functional mapping, and practical neuroimaging workflows.
